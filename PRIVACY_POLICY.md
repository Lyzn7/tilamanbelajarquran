## 1. Data We Collect and Use

### Data stored locally on your device

The App stores some information locally on your device to make the App work properly, including:

- App settings, such as theme, selected qari, font preferences, and download preferences.
- Reading state, such as bookmarks and last-read surah/ayah.
- Onboarding status.
- Cached Quran, doa, tafsir, schedule, and downloaded audio data for offline use.
- Ustadz AI daily usage count and reward quota status.
- Selected prayer schedule location, such as province and city/regency.

This local data is stored on your device and is not sent to our own server.

### Device location

With your permission, the App may access your device's approximate or precise location to detect your province and city/regency for prayer schedule and imsakiyah features. The App converts the device location into a schedule area, stores the selected area locally, and uses it to request prayer schedule data from supported schedule APIs.

The App does not continuously track your location in the background.

### Data you send when using Ustadz AI

When you use the Ustadz AI feature, your question and relevant conversation context are sent to Google Gemini API so the App can generate an answer. Do not submit sensitive personal information, private identity data, financial information, passwords, or other confidential information in Ustadz AI questions.

We use this data only to provide the AI response. The App does not provide medical, legal, financial, or official religious fatwa services. AI answers may be inaccurate, and users should verify important religious matters with qualified scholars or trusted institutions.

### Internet and API usage

The App connects to third-party APIs to provide Quran content, prayer schedules, Hijri calendar, doa, tafsir, search, AI answers, and ads. These third-party services may receive technical information such as IP address, device information, request metadata, and usage-related logs according to their own privacy practices.

### Advertising data

The App uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads, including ads that may give additional Ustadz AI usage quota after the user voluntarily watches a rewarded ad.

AdMob may collect or process device identifiers, IP address, ad interaction data, diagnostics, approximate location inferred from network information, and other advertising-related data for purposes such as ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, analytics, and reporting. The App requests non-personalized ads where implemented, but advertising SDKs may still process identifiers or technical data for operational and measurement purposes.

Learn more about Google's advertising privacy practices at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 2. How We Use Data

We use data for the following purposes:

- To provide Quran reading, audio playback, tafsir, doa, calendar, prayer schedule, and related app features.
- To save user preferences and reading progress.
- To detect and save the nearest prayer schedule area when you allow location access.
- To enable offline access for downloaded content.
- To process Ustadz AI questions and generate responses.
- To enforce the daily Ustadz AI usage limit and reward quota.
- To show rewarded ads and confirm when a reward should be granted.
- To improve reliability, prevent abuse, and maintain app functionality.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

Data may be shared or processed by third-party services used by the App, including:

- **Google Gemini API**, for generating Ustadz AI responses.
- **Google AdMob**, for advertising, rewarded ads, ad measurement, and fraud prevention.
- **Public Quran / Islamic content APIs**, for Quran, tafsir, doa, search, Hijri calendar, and prayer schedule features.

These third parties process data according to their own privacy policies and service terms.

## 4. Data Retention

Local app data remains on your device until:

- You clear the App's storage from your device settings.
- You uninstall the App.
- The App refreshes or replaces cached data.
- You delete downloaded content using available app controls, where provided.

Ustadz AI usage count is reset daily. Reward quota status is stored locally for the current day.

Data processed by third-party services is retained according to each third party's privacy policy and retention rules.
